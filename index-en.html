WELCOME TO MY ACADEMIC HOMEPAGE

Prospective PhD Applicant for Fall 2027

I am Liangdong Zhang, and I plan to apply for PhD programs beginning in Fall 2027. My research interests focus on physics-informed deep learning, hybrid physics–data-driven modeling, machining dynamics, and multisource information fusion for condition monitoring. I am currently pursuing an M.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering at Shandong University under the supervision of Professor Haifeng Ma and expect to graduate in 2027. During my graduate studies, I received the Chinese National Scholarship (Top 3%), the School Outstanding Achievement Award (ranked 1st in my cohort), and the First-Class Graduate Academic Scholarship of Shandong University.

Adaptive Grinding-Chatter Stability Monitoring Driven by Multisource Vibration Signals

Shandong Provincial Key R&D Program · Graduate Researcher

  • Developed an adaptive grinding-chatter stability monitoring method by integrating VMD-based feature decomposition, dynamic frequency-band tracking, and online decision-boundary updating.
  • Conducted multisource vibration sensing, information fusion, and causal analysis to reveal chatter propagation paths.
Sep. 2024 — Feb. 2026

High-Stability Grinding Control of Thin-Walled Ceramic Components

Shandong Provincial Key R&D Program · Graduate Researcher

  • Integrated grinding-wheel topography, abrasive-grain wear, undeformed chip thickness, and multistage material-removal mechanisms to establish a physics-informed grinding-force model.
  • Developed a PINN framework featuring hybrid physics–data losses and a dual-attention mechanism.
Sep. 2024 — Feb. 2026

Tool-Wear Prediction Under Varying Milling Conditions

Shandong Provincial Key R&D Program · Research Assistant · Supervisor: Professor Haifeng Ma

  • Proposed a dual-channel regression framework integrating multiscale convolution, BiLSTM, and multi-head attention to jointly model local wear fluctuations and long-term wear evolution.
  • Developed a weighted-sampling optimization strategy covering the complete tool life cycle, improving prediction stability and model sensitivity during late-stage wear.
  • Achieved average RMSE values of 5.26 μm and 2.98 μm on the HMoTP and PHM2010 datasets, respectively, reducing prediction error by approximately 34.3% compared with existing models.
Mar. 2022 — Jul. 2023

Intelligent Blind-Guiding Robot Based on Speed Adaptation and Visual Recognition

National College Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Undergraduate Researcher

  • Designed a wheel-legged blind-guiding robot and implemented adaptive human–robot speed control using Kalman filtering and cascade PID control.
  • Optimized the YOLOv5 visual-recognition module through transfer learning to enable traffic-signal detection and multimodal human–robot interaction.
Dec. 2021 — Dec. 2023

Intelligent High-Rise Glass Curtain-Wall Cleaning Robot

National College Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Undergraduate Researcher

  • Designed a solar-powered high-rise cleaning robot integrating negative-pressure adhesion, damped-roller cleaning, and fall-protection mechanisms.
